## Overview

The `Customer` entity represents an individual who arrives at the bank to complete a transaction. It tracks the complete lifecycle of a customer from arrival through service completion.

## Schema

<ResponseField name="id" type="string" required>
  Unique identifier for the customer, generated as a truncated UUID (8 characters).
  
  **Example:** `"c7f3a1b2"`
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="arrival_time" type="float" required>
  Simulation clock time (in seconds) when the customer arrived at the bank.
  
  **Example:** `3240.15` (54 minutes into simulation)
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="service_time" type="float" required>
  Duration (in seconds) required to complete the customer's transaction at the teller window.
  
  **Note:** This is determined stochastically based on the `service_config` distribution when the customer is generated.
  
  **Example:** `4.2` (4.2 seconds of service required)
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="priority" type="integer" required>
  Priority level assigned to the customer, affecting their position in the queue.
  
  **Values:**
  - `1` - HIGH priority (elderly, pregnant, disabled)
  - `2` - MEDIUM priority (preferred customers)
  - `3` - LOW priority (regular customers)
  
  **Note:** Lower numbers indicate higher priority. Customers are served in priority order.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="transaction_type" type="string" required>
  Type of banking transaction the customer needs to perform.
  
  **Values:**
  - `DEPOSIT` - Depositing money
  - `WITHDRAWAL` - Withdrawing money
  - `PAYMENT` - Making a payment
  - `INQUIRY` - Account inquiry or information request
  
  **Example:** `"DEPOSIT"`
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="status" type="string" default="WAITING">
  Current state of the customer in the system.
  
  **Values:**
  - `WAITING` - Customer is in queue waiting for service
  - `BEING_SERVED` - Customer is currently at a teller window
  - `COMPLETED` - Customer has finished service and left
  
  **Example:** `"WAITING"`
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="service_start_time" type="float | null" default={null}>
  Simulation clock time (in seconds) when the customer was called to a teller window. `null` if not yet served.
  
  **Example:** `3245.30`
  
  **Note:** The wait time can be calculated as: `service_start_time - arrival_time`
</ResponseField>

## Customer Lifecycle

A customer progresses through the following states:

```
ARRIVAL → WAITING → BEING_SERVED → COMPLETED
```

### 1. Arrival

- Customer is generated based on `arrival_config`
- Assigned random `priority` based on `priority_weights`
- Assigned random `transaction_type`
- Service time determined by `service_config`
- Status set to `WAITING`
- Added to queue

### 2. Waiting

- Customer remains in queue ordered by:
  1. Priority (ascending: 1, 2, 3)
  2. Arrival time (FIFO within same priority)
- `service_start_time` remains `null`

### 3. Being Served

- Customer is assigned to an available teller
- Status changes to `BEING_SERVED`
- `service_start_time` is recorded
- Service completion event scheduled for `service_start_time + service_time`

### 4. Completed

- Service finishes at scheduled time
- Status changes to `COMPLETED`
- Customer leaves the system
- Teller becomes available

## Calculated Metrics

From customer attributes, several metrics can be derived:

<ParamField path="wait_time" type="calculated">
  Time spent waiting in queue before service starts.
  
  **Formula:** `service_start_time - arrival_time`
  
  **Example:** `3245.30 - 3240.15 = 5.15 seconds`
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="total_time" type="calculated">
  Total time spent in the system (wait + service).
  
  **Formula:** `(service_start_time + service_time) - arrival_time`
  
  **Example:** `(3245.30 + 4.2) - 3240.15 = 9.35 seconds`
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="system_departure_time" type="calculated">
  Time when customer leaves the system.
  
  **Formula:** `service_start_time + service_time`
  
  **Example:** `3245.30 + 4.2 = 3249.50 seconds`
</ParamField>

## Priority Queue Ordering

Customers in the waiting queue are sorted by:

1. **Priority** (ascending) - Priority 1 customers are served before Priority 2 and 3
2. **Arrival time** (ascending) - Within the same priority, earlier arrivals are served first (FIFO)

### Example Queue Order

```
Position | ID       | Priority | Arrival Time | Transaction
---------|----------|----------|--------------|------------
1        | aaa111   | 1        | 3200.0       | WITHDRAWAL
2        | bbb222   | 1        | 3205.5       | PAYMENT
3        | ccc333   | 2        | 3190.0       | DEPOSIT
4        | ddd444   | 2        | 3210.0       | INQUIRY
5        | eee555   | 3        | 3180.0       | DEPOSIT
```

<Note>
  Even though customer `eee555` arrived first (3180.0), they are served last due to LOW priority (3). Customer `ccc333` has MEDIUM priority (2) but is served after HIGH priority customers despite arriving earlier.
</Note>

## Usage in API Responses

Customer objects appear in several API responses:

- **[GET /api/simulation/state](/api/simulation#get-simulation-state)** - Returns `waiting_queue` array of customers
- **[GET /api/tellers/state](/api/tellers#get-tellers-state)** - Returns `current_customer` for each busy teller
- **Metrics endpoints** - Customer data aggregated into statistics
